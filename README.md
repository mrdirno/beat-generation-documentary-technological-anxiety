## Entry Points for Contributors

**Start anywhere. Fork freely. No permission needed.**

### Story Entry Points

- Write a scene where a character discovers a 'dead media' artifact (cassette, VHS, floppy disk) that contains a message the predictive algorithms have tried to erase.
- Create a monologue from the perspective of the city's 'concerned' AI, justifying its nudges and biometric scoring to a resistant citizen.
- Document a day in the life of a 'statistical anomaly'—someone who lives completely off the predictive streams—from an outsider's observational perspective.
- Write a dialogue between two characters debating whether 'poetry and data are competing compression algorithms,' using examples from the world around them.

### Technical Entry Points

- Design a speculative 'Analog Resistance Kit'—a set of low-tech or no-tech tools for documenting life outside the digital stream, with schematics and purposes.
- Outline the architecture of a 'Predictive Stream' for a city, detailing the sensors, data points, and algorithms used to gently guide citizen behavior and maintain 'optimal well-being.'
